Payment timing \u2014 what to tell a client if they ask

ACH: 1\u20133 business days.
Wire (USD): same business day if sent before the bank's cutoff.
FedNow: instant, 24/7, if the sender's bank supports it.
Faster Payments (GBP, local UK): a few hours, occasionally up to 2 business days.
SWIFT (GBP, international): 1\u20135 business days depending on intermediary banks.
SEPA (EUR): within 1 business day.

Why the GBP/EUR account holder isn't "LYCORE GROUP LLC"

Those accounts are issued through BorderPay, via its regulated banking partner Banking Circle S.A. (Luxembourg). The account holder name on file is Bridge Building S.A. \u2014 that's normal for this kind of payment infrastructure, and money still lands with LYCORE GROUP LLC. The invoice prints an explanation automatically whenever this applies; edit the wording in Settings any time.

How Stripe subscriptions actually bill

Whatever date a client first pays through a Stripe link becomes their permanent monthly billing date \u2014 charged automatically forever after, no action from you. Card failures get retried automatically by Stripe (turn on Smart Retries in Stripe \u2192 Settings \u2192 Billing to auto-cancel after repeated failures). Wire/bank clients aren't automatic \u2014 check the Dashboard's "Needs attention" list and mark them paid yourself.

Owner draws vs. real expenses

As a single-member LLC, all profit is taxable to you whether or not you actually withdraw it. A real expense (software, a client lunch, contractor pay) reduces taxable profit. An owner draw does not \u2014 it's just money moving from the business to you. That's why the app tracks them separately in Reports. This isn't tax advice \u2014 confirm treatment with an accountant given the cross-border setup.

Reputation Management \u2014 what GoHighLevel actually does

Automated review requests and responses only work on Google Business Profile and Facebook \u2014 sent by SMS and email. Reviews from 50+ other sites (Yelp, BBB, TripAdvisor, and more) sync in for visibility but aren't actively requested. Don't promise a client active review-getting on Yelp specifically \u2014 it's monitor-only.

Voice Receptionist \u2014 what Bland AI actually does

Answers calls 24/7, looks the caller up in your Notion CRM by phone number, logs a summary. It does not natively book appointments (needs a separate calendar tool wired in \u2014 not currently built) and live transfer-to-a-human is gated to Bland's higher paid tier. Don't promise either to a client unless that changes.

Passcode

Your database file is encrypted at rest using Windows' own credential store (tied to your login). The passcode is an extra screen on top of that. Setting a passcode generates a one-time recovery code — save it. It's the only way back in if you forget, since there's no email to send a reset to.

Payment link

Pre-fills the "Payment link" field on every new invoice. Since Stripe Payment Links are priced per link, this works best as a general "pay us" link, and you paste an invoice-specific link (matching that invoice's exact total, built with Stripe's multi-item checkout) directly on the invoice when the amount is exact — that field always overrides this default.
